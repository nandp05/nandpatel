About

I'm Nand Patel, a commercial real estate advisor with Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Florida Properties Group in Tampa Bay. Florida Licensed Real Estate Sales Associate (FL Lic. SL3655318), I focus on retail investment sales — strip centers, plazas, gas stations, liquor stores, and NNN retail.
